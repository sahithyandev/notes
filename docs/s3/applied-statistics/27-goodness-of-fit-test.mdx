Used to check whether a set of observed categorical data follows a theoretical distribution such as uniform or Poisson.

## Hypotheses

$H_0$: Data follow the specified distribution.

$H_1$: Data do not follow the specified distribution.

## Test Statistic

$$
\chi^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \frac{(O_i - E_i)^2}{E_i} \quad \sim \chi^2_\nu
$$

Here:

- $O_i$: observed frequency for class $i$
- $E_i$: expected frequency for class $i$ under $H_0$
- $k$: number of non-empty classes

## Degrees of Freedom

$$
\nu = k - p - 1
$$

Here:

- $p$: number of parameters estimated from the data

## Decision Rule

Reject $H_0$ if $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{\nu, \alpha}$ (right-tail test).

## Small Expected Frequencies

When any $E_i < 5$, merge adjacent classes until all $E_i \geq 5$. Recalculate $k$ and $\nu$ after merging.

<Note>

$E_i$ is computed as $n \cdot p_i$ where $p_i$ is the theoretical probability of class $i$ and $n$ is the total sample size.

</Note>
