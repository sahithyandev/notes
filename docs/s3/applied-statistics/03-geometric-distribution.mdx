A special case of the [negative binomial distribution](/s3/applied-statistics/pascal-distribution/), where $r=1$. Denoted by $g^*(x;p)$. [Memoryless](/s3/applied-statistics/introduction/#memorylessness).

$$
P(x) = p(1-p)^{x-1}
$$

Here:

- $p$ - probability of success in a single trial

### Mean

$$
\mu_X = \frac{1}{p}
$$

### Variance

$$
V_x = \frac{1-p}{p^2}
$$
