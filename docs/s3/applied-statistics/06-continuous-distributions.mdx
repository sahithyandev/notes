## Normal Distribution

Denoted by $N(\mu, \sigma^2)$.

$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} \exp\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)
$$

Properties:

- Symmetric about $\mu$
- Kurtosis is 3
- CDF has a sigmoid (S-shaped) curve; no closed form; use tables or software

### When to Use

Applies when the Central Limit Theorem (CLT) holds: sample means from any distribution with finite variance approach normality as $n \to \infty$. Rule of thumb: $n \geq 30$.

## Standard Normal Distribution

Denoted by $N(0, 1)$. Variable is written as $z$.

$$
\phi(z) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \exp\left(-\frac{z^2}{2}\right)
$$

### Standardization

Any $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$ can be converted to $z$:

$$
z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}
$$

Standardization enables use of a single $z$-table for all normal distributions.

Other continuous distributions are explained in separate pages, in detail.
