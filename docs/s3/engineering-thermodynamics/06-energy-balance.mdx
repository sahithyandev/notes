Based on [the 1st law](/s3/engineering-thermodynamics/1st-law), more specific equations are derived on a case by case basis.

### Transient State

When a thermodynamic system's state and energy transfer changes with time.

### Steady State

When a thermodynamic system's state constants with time. Net energy change in the system is zero.

## For Closed Systems

Net energy transfer to a closed system is equal to the net increase in the system's total energy.

$$
\begin{equation}
\nonumber
\begin{split}
\Delta Q - \Delta W & = \Delta E_{\text{sys}} = \Delta U + \Delta KE + \Delta PE \\
\Delta U & = m(h_2 - h_1) \\
\Delta KE & = \frac{1}{2}m \left(C_2^2 - C_1^2\right) \\
\Delta PE & = mg(z_2 - z_1)
\end{split}
\end{equation}
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="\Delta Q">net heat transfer to the system</Sym>
  <Sym s="\Delta W">net work transfer from the system</Sym>
  <Sym s="\Delta E_{\text{sys}}">change in total energy</Sym>
  <Sym s="\Delta U">change in internal energy</Sym>
  <Sym s="\Delta KE">change in kinetic energy</Sym>
  <Sym s="\Delta PE">change in potential energy</Sym>
  <Sym s="h_1, h_2">specific enthalpy</Sym>
  <Sym s="m">mass of the system</Sym>
  <Sym s="C_1, C_2">velocity of the system</Sym>
  <Sym s="g">gravitational acceleration</Sym>
  <Sym s="z_1, z_2">elevation of the system</Sym>
</Symbols>

## For Open Systems

Total specific energy of a flow system:

$$
\theta = h + \frac{1}{2}C^2 + gz
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="\theta">total specific energy</Sym>
  <Sym s="h = u + P\nu">enthalpy</Sym>
  <Sym s="u">specific internal energy</Sym>
  <Sym s="P\nu">specific flow work</Sym>
  <Sym s="C">velocity of the flow</Sym>
  <Sym s="g">gravatitaional acceleration</Sym>
  <Sym s="z">elevation of the flow system</Sym>
</Symbols>

$$
Q - W + m_{\text{in}}\theta_{\text{in}} - m_{\text{out}}\theta_{\text{out}} = \Delta E_{\text{sys}}
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="Q">heat transfer</Sym>
  <Sym s="W">work transfer</Sym>
  <Sym s="m_{\text{in}}">mass flow rate of the inlet</Sym>
  <Sym s="\theta_{\text{in}}">total specific energy of the inlet</Sym>
  <Sym s="m_{\text{out}}">mass flow rate of the outlet</Sym>
  <Sym s="\theta_{\text{out}}">total specific energy of the outlet</Sym>
  <Sym s="\Delta E_{\text{sys}}">change in total energy of the system</Sym>
</Symbols>

### Steady Flow Energy Equation (SFEE)

Used when an open system is in steady state.

$$
q - w = h_e - h_i + \frac{1}{2}\Big(C_e^2 - C_i^2\Big) + g(z_e - z_i)
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="q">specific heat transfer</Sym>
  <Sym s="w">specific work transfer</Sym>
  <Sym s="h_e, h_i">specific enthalpy at the outlet and inlet</Sym>
  <Sym s="C_e, C_i">flow velocity at the outlet and inlet</Sym>
  <Sym s="g">gravitational acceleration</Sym>
  <Sym s="z_e,z_i">elevation at the outlet and inlet</Sym>
</Symbols>
