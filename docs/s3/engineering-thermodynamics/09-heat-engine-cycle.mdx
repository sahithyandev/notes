### Efficiency

$$
\eta_\text{thermal} = \frac{W_\text{net}}{Q_\text{in}} = 1 - \frac{Q_\text{out}}{Q_\text{in}}
$$

## Carnot Cycle

Ideal reversible cycle for a heat engine. Has maximum thermal efficiency for given temperature limits.

Has 4 processes. All processes are reversible.

- Through the boiler  
  Isothermal heat addition.
- Through the turbine  
  Adiabatic expansion.
- Through the condenser  
  Isothermal heat rejection.
- Through the pump  
  Adiabatic compression.

### Carnot Efficiency

$$
\eta_\text{Carnot} = 1 - \frac{T_L}{T_H}
$$

Here:

- $T_L$: temperature of the cold reservoir (K)
- $T_H$: temperature of the hot reservoir (K)

No real engine can exceed Carnot efficiency operating between the same temperature limits.

## Coefficient of Performance

For reverse heat engines (refrigerators and heat pumps), performance is expressed as Coefficient of Performance (COP) rather than efficiency, since COP can exceed 1.

### Refrigerator

$$
\text{COP}_R = \frac{Q_L}{W_\text{net,in}} = \frac{Q_L}{Q_H - Q_L}
$$

Carnot COP for refrigerator:

$$
\text{COP}_{R,\text{Carnot}} = \frac{T_L}{T_H - T_L}
$$

### Heat Pump

$$
\text{COP}_{HP} = \frac{Q_H}{W_\text{net,in}} = \frac{Q_H}{Q_H - Q_L}
$$

Relation between the 2 COP values:

$$
\text{COP}_{HP} = \text{COP}_R + 1
$$
