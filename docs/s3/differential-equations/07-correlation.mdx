Correlation between $f$ and $g$, 2 piecewise continuous functions absolutely integrable over $(-\infty, \infty)$:

$$
(f \star g)(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(\lambda) g(\lambda - t) \, \text{d}\lambda
$$

## Relation with Convolution

$$
(f \star g)(t) = (-f * g)(t) = (f * {-g})(t)
$$

## Types

### Cross-Correlation

Correlation of 2 different signals $f$ and $g$. Measures the similarity between them as a function of time lag $t$. Not commutative in general:

$$
(f \star g)(t) \neq (g \star f)(t)
$$

### Autocorrelation

Correlation of a signal with itself:

$$
R_{ff}(t) = (f \star f)(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(\lambda) f(\lambda - t) \, \text{d}\lambda
$$

Always symmetric: $R_{ff}(t) = R_{ff}(-t)$.

## Applications

- Signal detection: finding a known pattern within a noisy signal by sliding it over the signal
- Time-delay estimation: determining the lag between 2 related signals from different sensors
- Power spectral density: autocorrelation and PSD are a Fourier transform pair (Wiener-Khinchin theorem)
