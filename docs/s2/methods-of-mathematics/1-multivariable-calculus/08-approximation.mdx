**If** $f(x,y)$ is differentiable at $(x_0,y_0)$ **then**:

$$
f(x,y) \approx f(x_0,y_0) + f_x(x_0,y_0)\,\Delta x + f_y(x_0,y_0)\,\Delta y
$$

## Total Differential

For $z = f(x,y)$, the total differential is:

$$
dz = f_x(x,y)\,\text{d}x + f_y(x,y)\,\text{d}y
$$

$dz$ gives the exact change in the tangent plane for increments $dx$ and $dy$. It approximates $\Delta z = f(x+dx, y+dy) - f(x,y)$ when $dx$ and $dy$ are small.

## Example

Approximate $f(2.01,\, 3.98)$ where $f(x,y) = x^2 + xy$.

At $(x_0, y_0) = (2, 4)$:

- $f(2,4) = 4 + 8 = 12$
- $f_x = 2x + y \Rightarrow f_x(2,4) = 8$
- $f_y = x \Rightarrow f_y(2,4) = 2$

$$
\Delta f \approx 8(0.01) + 2(-0.02) = 0.08 - 0.04 = 0.04
$$

$$
f(2.01,\, 3.98) \approx 12.04
$$
