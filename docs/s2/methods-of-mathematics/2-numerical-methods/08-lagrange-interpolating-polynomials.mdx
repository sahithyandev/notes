A rearranged version of Newton's Divided Difference Interpolating Polynomial.

$$
L_{n,k}(x) = \prod_{i=0,i\neq k}^{n} \frac{x - x_i}{x_k - x_i}
$$

$n$-th Lagrange interpolating polynomial is:

$$
P_n(x)=
\sum_{k=0}^n
L_{n,k}(x)f(x_k)
$$
