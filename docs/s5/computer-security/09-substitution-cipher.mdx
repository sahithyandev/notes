A [classical cipher](/s5/computer-security/ciphers#classical-ciphers), from the pre-computer era.

The key is a proper permutation of the plaintext alphabet, rather than a simple shift.

The key space equals the number of permutations of the alphabet, $26! \approx 4.03 \times 10^{26} \approx 2^{88}$.

<Note>

A massive key space does not make the substitution cipher unbreakable. Statistical techniques based on language characteristics still apply.

</Note>

You can find an implementation of the substitution cipher in [sahithyandev/ciphers](https://github.com/sahithyandev/ciphers/blob/main/src/substitution-cipher.c).
