Total production cost is the summation of prime cost and manufacturing overhead.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Prime Cost} = \ &\text{Direct Material Cost} \\
+ \ &\text{Direct Labour Cost} \\
+ \ &\text{Direct Other Cost}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{Total Production Cost} = \text{Prime Cost} + \text{Production Overhead Cost}
$$

## Cost Structure

- Manufacturing / production cost  
  Direct material, direct labour, variable manufacturing overhead, fixed manufacturing overhead.
- Non-manufacturing / non-production cost  
  Variable non-manufacturing overhead, fixed non-manufacturing overhead. Any expense incurred after completion of manufacturing, or not incurred for production.

<Note>

For an electric car as the cost object: direct material is the battery pack, motor, and body panels. Direct labour is assembly and battery installation technicians. Variable manufacturing overhead is electricity for robotic assembly lines and welding gas. Fixed manufacturing overhead is factory rent and the production manager's salary. Variable non-manufacturing overhead is admin office electricity and sales commission. Fixed non-manufacturing overhead is administrative salaries and office depreciation.

</Note>
