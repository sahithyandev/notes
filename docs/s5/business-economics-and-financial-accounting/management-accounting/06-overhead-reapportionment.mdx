Service department costs are reapportioned to operating departments, since [service departments](/s5/business-economics-and-financial-accounting/management-accounting/overhead-allocation-and-apportionment) do not carry out production directly but support it.

## Reapportionment Basis

- Purchase department  
  Number of purchase orders, or value of materials purchased for each production department.
- Stores  
  Value or quantity of materials issued, or number of materials requisitions.
- Internal transport  
  Weight and distance.
- Inspection  
  Inspection hours devoted to each department.

## Methods

### Direct Method

Costs of service departments are apportioned directly to production departments, without considering any service from one service department to another. Proper apportionment cannot be done, so production departments may be overcharged or undercharged.

Good when service departments do not provide service to other service departments.

### Step Down Method

Best used where some service cost centres provide services to other service cost centres, but these services are not reciprocated.

### Reciprocal Method

Used where service departments provide services to each other reciprocally.

#### Simultaneous Equation Method

Sets up the overhead reapportionment process as a set of equations, letting $A$ be the total overhead apportioned to department $A$, $B$ the total overhead apportioned to department $B$, and so on.

#### Repeated Distribution Method

Each service department's total cost is repeatedly reallocated among other departments, based on a given percentage basis, until the cost of each service department becomes negligible.

## Worked Examples

Consider production departments $P_1$ and $P_2$, and service departments $S_1$ and $S_2$, with overheads before reapportionment: $P_1 = 10000$, $P_2 = 8000$, $S_1 = 4000$, $S_2 = 2000$.

### Direct Method

$S_1$ serves $P_1$ 60% and $P_2$ 40%. $S_2$ serves $P_1$ 50% and $P_2$ 50%.

$
\begin{aligned}
S_1 \to P_1 &= 0.60 \times 4000 = 2400 \\
S_1 \to P_2 &= 0.40 \times 4000 = 1600 \\
S_2 \to P_1 &= 0.50 \times 2000 = 1000 \\
S_2 \to P_2 &= 0.50 \times 2000 = 1000
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
P_1 &= 10000 + 2400 + 1000 = 13400 \\
P_2 &= 8000 + 1600 + 1000 = 10600
\end{aligned}
$

### Step Down Method

$S_1$ is apportioned first, serving $S_2$ 10%, $P_1$ 60%, $P_2$ 30%.

$
\begin{aligned}
S_1 \to S_2 &= 0.10 \times 4000 = 400 \\
S_1 \to P_1 &= 0.60 \times 4000 = 2400 \\
S_1 \to P_2 &= 0.30 \times 4000 = 1200
\end{aligned}
$

$S_2
s cost then includes what it received from $S_1$, and is apportioned to $P_1$ and $P_2$ only, without going back to $S_1$. $S_2
s total becomes $2000 + 400 = 2400$, split $P_1$ 50%, $P_2$ 50%.

$
\begin{aligned}
S_2 \to P_1 &= 0.50 \times 2400 = 1200 \\
S_2 \to P_2 &= 0.50 \times 2400 = 1200
\end{aligned}
$

$
\begin{aligned}
P_1 &= 10000 + 2400 + 1200 = 13600 \\
P_2 &= 8000 + 1200 + 1200 = 10400
\end{aligned}
$

### Reciprocal Method

Uses $S_1$ serving $S_2$ 10%, $P_1$ 60%, $P_2$ 30%, and $S_2$ serving $S_1$ 20%, $P_1$ 40%, $P_2$ 40%.

#### Simultaneous Equation Method

$
\begin{aligned}
S_1 &= 4000 + 0.20 S_2 \\
S_2 &= 2000 + 0.10 S_1
\end{aligned}
$

Substitute $S_2$ into $S_1$ and solve.

$
S_1 = 4000 + 0.20(2000 + 0.10 S_1) = 4400 + 0.02 S_1
$

$
0.98 S_1 = 4400 \implies S_1 = 4489.80
$

$
S_2 = 2000 + 0.10 \times 4489.80 = 2448.98
$

$
\begin{aligned}
P_1 &= 10000 + 0.60(4489.80) + 0.40(2448.98) = 13673.47 \\
P_2 &= 8000 + 0.30(4489.80) + 0.40(2448.98) = 10326.53
\end{aligned}
$

#### Repeated Distribution Method

Uses the same percentages as the Simultaneous Equation Method.

- Round 1  
  Distribute $S_1 = 4000$: $S_2$ gains $400$, $P_1$ gains $2400$, $P_2$ gains $1200$.
  Distribute $S_2 = 2400$: $S_1$ gains $480$, $P_1$ gains $960$, $P_2$ gains $960$.
- Round 2  
  Distribute $S_1 = 480$: $S_2$ gains $48$, $P_1$ gains $288$, $P_2$ gains $144$.
  Distribute $S_2 = 48$: $S_1$ gains $9.6$, $P_1$ gains $19.2$, $P_2$ gains $19.2$.
- Round 3  
  $S_1 = 9.6$ is negligible, so distribution stops.

$
\begin{aligned}
P_1 &\approx 10000 + 2400 + 960 + 288 + 19.2 = 13667.2 \\
P_2 &\approx 8000 + 1200 + 960 + 144 + 19.2 = 10323.2
\end{aligned}
$

Further rounds converge these towards the Simultaneous Equation Method's result.
