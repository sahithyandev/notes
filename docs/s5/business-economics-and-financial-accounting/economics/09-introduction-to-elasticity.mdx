## Elasticity

Elasticity measures the **sensitivity or responsiveness of one variable
to a change in another variable**.

There are four types of elasticity:

- Price elasticity of demand
- Income elasticity of demand
- Cross elasticity of demand
- Price elasticity of supply

## Role of Price

Price:

- Measures relative scarcity.
- Provides incentives to use scarce resources efficiently.
- Communicates important economic information.
