Supply is the quantity of a good or service that producers are
willing to sell at different prices during a given period.

Market supply is the sum of the quantities supplied by all individual
producers.

## Supply Schedule

A supply schedule is a table showing the quantities producers are
willing to supply at different prices.

As price increases, quantity supplied generally increases.

## Law of Supply

Holding other factors constant, when the price of a product increases,
quantity supplied increases. When the price decreases, quantity
supplied decreases.

Therefore, price and quantity supplied are positively related.

## Factors of Supply

Supply depends on price and on a set of non-price factors.

### Input Costs

Input costs are the prices of factors of production.

When input costs increase, supply decreases.

### Size of the Industry

When more firms enter the industry, supply increases.

### Technology

When technology improves, production costs fall, so supply increases.

### Prices of Other Goods

When the price of another good that producers could make instead rises, producers may switch resources to it, so supply of this good decreases.

### Taxation

When taxes on production increase, costs rise, so supply decreases.

### Goals of Firms

Firms that pursue goals other than profit maximization, such as sales growth or market share, may supply different quantities at each price.

## Supply Curve

A supply curve shows the relationship between price and quantity
supplied.

It generally slopes upward from left to right.

Producers are willing to supply more at higher prices because of
the opportunity to earn greater profits.

A change in the price of the good itself moves the economy along the
same supply curve. A change in any non-price determinant shifts the
entire curve.

### Change in Quantity Supplied

A change in quantity supplied occurs because of a change in the
price of the good itself. Causes a movement along the same supply curve.

When price increases, quantity supplied increases. When price decreases, quantity supplied decreases.

### Change in Supply

A change in supply occurs because of a change in a non-price
determinant. Causes a shift of the entire supply curve.

An increase in supply shifts the supply curve right. A decrease in supply shifts the supply curve left.
