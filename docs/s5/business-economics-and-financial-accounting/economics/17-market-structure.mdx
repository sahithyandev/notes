A market structure describes how competition works in an industry and how much control firms have over price.

Classify a market by counting firms, comparing products, checking entry barriers, and asking how much control firms have over price.

## Competition

Competition is the effort of firms to attract customers and achieve their objectives.

The 2 broad types are:

- Perfect competition
  <Aka>Pure Competition</Aka>.
- Imperfect competition  
  Firms have some control over price. It includes monopolistic competition, oligopoly, and monopoly.

Each type has a separate note.

## Market Power

Market power is the ability to raise price by reducing output without losing every customer.

## Barriers to Entry

Barriers to entry make it difficult for new firms to enter a market.

Examples:

- High start-up costs
- Technological requirements
- Patents
- Control over important resources
- Legal restrictions
