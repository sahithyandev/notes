A market structure is the set of characteristics that determine the level of competition and pricing power within a market.

## Competition

Competition is the effort of firms to attract customers and achieve their objectives.

2 types:

- Perfect Competition
  <Aka>Pure Competition</Aka>.
- Imperfect Competition  
  Markets where firms have some control over price because of factors such as product differentiation or a small number of competitors. Includes:
  - Monopolistic Competition
  - Oligopoly
  - Monopoly

Each are explained in depth on their own pages.

## Barriers to Entry

Factors that make it difficult for new firms to enter a market.

Examples:

- High start-up costs
- Technological requirements
- Patents
- Control over important resources
- Legal restrictions
