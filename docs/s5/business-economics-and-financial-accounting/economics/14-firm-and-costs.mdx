## The Firm

A firm is an institution that hires factors of production and organizes them to produce and sell goods and services.

The main goal of a firm is to **maximize profit**.

$$
\text{Profit} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Cost}
$$

## Types of Costs

### Explicit Costs

Costs that involve an actual monetary payment.

Examples:

- Wages
- Raw materials
- Rent
- Interest on borrowed funds

### Implicit Costs

Non-monetary opportunity costs of using resources owned by the firm or owner.

Examples:

- Salary the owner gives up by running the business
- Interest the owner gives up by using personal savings

## Economic Profit and Accounting Profit

### Accounting Profit

$$
\text{Accounting Profit}
=
\text{Total Revenue} - \text{Explicit Costs}
$$

### Economic Profit

$$
\text{Economic Profit}
=
\text{Total Revenue}
-
(\text{Explicit Costs} + \text{Implicit Costs})
$$

Economic profit is less than or equal to accounting profit because it includes implicit costs.

When economic profit is zero, the firm earns a **normal profit**.
