Elasticity measures the sensitivity or responsiveness of one variable
to a change in another variable.

Example: if a 10% price increase causes quantity demanded to fall by 20%, demand responds twice as strongly as price.

The 4 types are:

- Price elasticity of demand
- Income elasticity of demand
- Cross elasticity of demand
- Price elasticity of supply

## Role of Price

Price performs 3 functions:

- Rationing  
  A higher price limits demand when a good is scarce.
- Incentive  
  A higher price encourages producers to supply more.
- Information  
  A price change signals changing scarcity or demand.

Example: a higher hotel price during a festival rations limited rooms and encourages owners to offer more rooms.
