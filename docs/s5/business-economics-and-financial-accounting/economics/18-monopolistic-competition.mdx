Monopolistic competition is a market structure with many firms selling products that are similar but differentiated.

## Conditions

### Many Firms

There are many firms competing for customers.

### Few Artificial Barriers to Entry

New firms can enter relatively easily.

### Slight Control over Price

Product differentiation gives firms some control over their prices.

### Differentiated Products

Products differ in characteristics such as quality, design, taste, service, or brand image.

## Non-Price Competition

Firms attract customers without simply lowering their prices.

Four common methods are:

1. **Characteristics of goods**  
   Size, colour, shape, texture, or taste.

2. **Location**  
   A convenient location can attract customers.

3. **Service level**  
   Better service can allow a firm to charge a higher price.

4. **Advertising and image**  
   Advertising creates differences between competing products.

## Perfect vs Monopolistic Competition

| Characteristic  | Perfect Competition | Monopolistic Competition |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Number of firms | Many                | Many                     |
| Product         | Identical           | Differentiated           |
| Price control   | None                | Some                     |
| Entry           | Easy                | Relatively easy          |
| Variety         | Low                 | High                     |
| Competition     | Mainly price        | Price and non-price      |
