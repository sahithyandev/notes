Price elasticity of supply measures the responsiveness of quantity
supplied to a change in price.

$$
E_s =
\frac{\%\text{ change in quantity supplied}}
{\%\text{ change in price}}
$$

### Categories of Price Elasticity of Supply

| Type                    | Elasticity | Meaning                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Elastic Supply**      | $E_s > 1$  | Quantity supplied changes by a larger percentage than price  |
| **Inelastic Supply**    | $E_s < 1$  | Quantity supplied changes by a smaller percentage than price |
| **Unit Elastic Supply** | $E_s = 1$  | Quantity supplied changes by the same percentage as price    |

## Worked Examples

### Elasticities and a Price Rise

For a good, $E_d = -2$ and $E_s = 1$. The price rises by 10%. Find the changes in quantity demanded and quantity supplied.

<Solution>

$$
\%\Delta Q_d = -2 \times 10\% = -20\%
$$

$$
\%\Delta Q_s = 1 \times 10\% = +10\%
$$

Quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded, so there is a [surplus](/s5/business-economics-and-financial-accounting/economics/market-equilibrium#surplus) that puts downward pressure on price.

</Solution>
