Demand is the quantity of a good or service that consumers are
willing and able to purchase at various prices during a given period.

## Demand Schedule

A demand schedule is a table showing the quantities consumers are
willing and able to purchase at different prices.

As price falls, quantity demanded generally increases.

## Law of Demand

Holding other factors constant, when the price of a good or service
increases, quantity demanded decreases. When the price decreases,
quantity demanded increases.

Therefore, price and quantity demanded are inversely related.

The law holds because of two effects.

### Substitution Effect

The response to a change in relative price, with other things constant.

When the price of a good increases, consumers tend to substitute
relatively cheaper alternatives.

### Income Effect

The response to a change in real income, with relative prices constant.

When the price of a good increases, the consumer's purchasing power
or real income decreases, so the consumer may buy less of the good.

## Factors of Demand

Demand depends on price and on a set of non-price factors.

### Prices of Related Goods

A related good is either:

- A substitute  
  When price of a substitute increases, demand for the original increases.  
  Example: coffee's price increase causes tea's demand increase
- A complement  
  When price of a complement increases, demand for the original decreases.  
  Example: car's price increase causes petrol's demand decrease

### Number of Consumers

The number of consumers in the market is the population of potential buyers.

When population increases, demand increases.

### Consumer Income

For a normal good, the quantity demanded rises as income rises at each price.

When income increases, demand increases.

### Consumer Tastes and Preferences

When a product becomes more fashionable or desirable, demand increases.

When it falls out of fashion, demand decreases.

### Expectations About the Future

Consumers' expectations about future prices and income change how much they buy now.

If consumers expect the price or their income to rise, demand now increases.

### Advertising and Other

Advertising increases awareness of a good and can raise its perceived value, which increases demand.

## Demand Curve

A demand curve shows the relationship between the price of a good
and the quantity demanded.

It normally slopes downward from left to right.

- Price on the vertical axis.
- Quantity demanded on the horizontal axis.

### Horizontal Demand Curve

A horizontal demand curve means consumers will buy any quantity at a
specific price, but zero quantity at any higher price.

### Change in Quantity Demanded

A change in quantity demanded occurs because of a change in the
price of the good itself. Causes a movement along the same demand curve. When price increases, quantity demanded decreases.

### Change in Demand

A change in demand occurs because of a change in a non-price
determinant. Causes a shift of the entire demand curve.
