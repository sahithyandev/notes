## Oligopoly

An oligopoly is a market structure where a **few large firms** dominate the industry.

Products may be:

- Identical
- Differentiated

There are usually significant barriers to entry.

## Collusion

Collusion occurs when firms in an oligopoly cooperate to influence prices or production.

### Price Fixing

An agreement among firms to sell at the same or similar prices.

### Cartel

An association of producers formed to coordinate prices and production.

Oligopolies may also engage in **price wars**, where firms compete by reducing prices.

## Market Power

Market power is the ability of a firm to control prices and output.

Generally:

- Monopoly and oligopoly → greater market power
- Monopolistic and perfect competition → less market power

Markets with greater market power tend to have higher prices and lower output.

## Predatory Pricing

Predatory pricing occurs when a firm sets its price below cost for a short period to drive competitors out of the market.

After competitors leave, the firm may increase its price.

## Government and Competition

Governments may use competition policies to prevent firms from gaining excessive market power.

### Anti-Trust Laws

Anti-trust laws are designed to encourage competition and limit monopoly or oligopoly power.

Four forms include:

1. **Regulating business practices**  
   Government can intervene when a firm has excessive market power.

2. **Breaking up monopolies**  
   Monopolies may be broken up to increase competition.

3. **Blocking mergers**  
   A merger may be blocked if it significantly reduces competition.

4. **Preserving incentives**  
   Mergers may be allowed when they provide benefits to consumers.

## Deregulation

Deregulation is the removal of some government controls over a market.

It is intended to promote competition.

Possible effects include:

- More competition
- Lower prices
- Greater variety
- Possible short-term job losses or business closures

## Four Market Structures - Summary

| Characteristic  | Perfect Competition | Monopolistic Competition | Oligopoly                   | Monopoly  |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------- | --------- |
| Number of firms | Many                | Many                     | Few                         | One       |
| Product         | Identical           | Differentiated           | Identical or differentiated | Unique    |
| Entry           | High                | High                     | Low                         | Blocked   |
| Price control   | None                | Some                     | Considerable                | High      |
| Market power    | Very low            | Low                      | High                        | Very high |
