## Total Revenue

Total revenue is the amount paid by buyers and received by sellers.

$$
TR = P \times Q
$$

Where:

- **TR** = Total Revenue
- **P** = Price
- **Q** = Quantity sold

## Price Effect and Quantity Effect

When a firm changes its price, two effects occur:

### Price Effect

A higher price means each unit sold generates more revenue.

### Quantity Effect

A higher price usually causes fewer units to be sold, reducing revenue.

The price elasticity of demand determines which effect is stronger.

| Demand                    | Price Increase          | Price Decrease          |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Elastic** (Ed > 1)      | Total revenue decreases | Total revenue increases |
| **Inelastic** (Ed < 1)    | Total revenue increases | Total revenue decreases |
| **Unit Elastic** (Ed = 1) | Total revenue unchanged | Total revenue unchanged |

## Key Point

Total revenue is maximized when demand is **unit elastic**.

$$
|E_d| = 1
$$

Therefore:

- **Elastic demand** → Price and total revenue move in opposite directions.
- **Inelastic demand** → Price and total revenue move in the same direction.
- **Unit elastic demand** → A change in price does not change total revenue.
