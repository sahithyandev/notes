Comparative analysis evaluates and compares a company's financial performance and position across multiple periods, typically consecutive years, to identify trends, changes, and patterns.

Amounts for 2 or more years are placed side by side along with the change in absolute and percentage terms.

## Types

- Intra company basis  
  Comparison within the same company across periods.
- Inter company basis  
  Comparison against another company.
- Industry averages  
  Comparison against industry benchmarks.
