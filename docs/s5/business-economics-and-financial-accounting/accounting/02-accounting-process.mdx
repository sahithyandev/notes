- Identification  
  Recognize and analyze financial transactions, preparing source documents to extract financial information.
- Classification  
  Group similar transactions into categories, posted in the general ledger.
- Recording  
  Document transactions systematically in journals and ledgers.
- Summarization  
  Prepare trial balances and financial statements.
- Interpretation  
  Analyze financial data to support decision-making, turning raw financial information into usable insight.

## Prime Entry Books

### Source Document

The original record evidencing a transaction, used as the basis for the first entry into the accounting system.

### Journal

The book of prime entry where transactions are recorded chronologically from source documents, before posting to the ledger.

| Source document                | Journal                        | Purpose                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Purchase invoice               | Purchases journal              | Record credit purchases of goods              |
| Sales invoice                  | Sales journal                  | Record credit sales of goods                  |
| Debit note                     | Purchases return journal       | Record goods returned to suppliers            |
| Credit note                    | Sales return journal           | Record goods returned by customers            |
| Cash receipt / payment voucher | Cash receipt / payment journal | Record cash receipts and payments             |
| Journal voucher                | General journal                | Record entries not covered by the other books |

## Secondary Entry Books

### Account

A record that tracks increases and decreases in a specific asset, liability, equity, income, or expense item.

### Ledger

The book where transactions are posted from journals and grouped by account, showing the running balance of each account.
