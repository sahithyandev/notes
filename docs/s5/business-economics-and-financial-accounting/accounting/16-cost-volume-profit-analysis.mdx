<Aka>breakeven analysis</Aka>. The study of the interrelationships between
costs, volume, and profit at various levels of activity.

## Contribution per Unit

Difference between selling price per unit and variable cost per unit.

$$
\text{Contribution} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Variable Cost}
$$

## Contribution to Sales Ratio

<Aka>the profit/volume ratio</Aka>. Measures how much contribution is earned
from each unit of sales.

$$
\text{C/S Ratio} = \frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100\%
$$

## Breakeven Point

The level of activity at which there is neither profit nor loss. Total revenue equals total cost.

$$
\text{BEP (units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{Contribution per Unit}}
$$

$$
\text{BEP (Sales Value)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{C/S Ratio}}
$$

## Margin of Safety

The extent by which actual or projected sales exceed breakeven sales.

$$
\text{Margin of Safety} = \text{Budgeted Sales} - \text{Breakeven Sales}
$$

## Breakeven Chart

A graph plotting total cost and total revenue against volume of activity.

The fixed cost line runs horizontal. Total cost line starts at fixed cost and rises with variable cost per unit. Sales revenue line starts at the origin and rises with selling price per unit. The breakeven point is the intersection point of total cost and sales revenue lines. Margin of safety is the horizontal distance between that point and actual or budgeted sales.

## Limitations

- Applies only to a single product or a single mix of a group of products.
- Assumes fixed costs, variable costs, sales prices are constant at all levels of output.
- Assumes production and sales are equal, ignoring inventory levels.
- Ignores the uncertainty in the estimates of fixed costs and variable cost per unit.

## Worked Example

A product sells for $50$ per unit with variable cost of $30$ per unit. Fixed costs are $400000$ per period. Budgeted sales are $25000$ units.

Find the breakeven point in units and sales value, the margin of safety, the units required for a target profit of $120000$, and the profit at budgeted sales.

<Solution>

Contribution per unit and the C/S ratio:

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
\text{Contribution per Unit} & = & 50 - 30 & = 20 \\
\text{C/S Ratio} & = & \dfrac{20}{50} \times 100 & = 40\%
\end{array}
$$

Breakeven point in units and sales value:

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
\text{BEP (units)} & = & \dfrac{400000}{20} & = 20000 \\
\text{BEP (Sales Value)} & = & \dfrac{400000}{0.40} & = 1000000
\end{array}
$$

Margin of safety, from budgeted sales of $25000 \times 50 = 1250000$:

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
\text{Margin of Safety} & = & 1250000 - 1000000 & = 250000 \\
\text{Margin of Safety (units)} & = & 25000 - 20000 & = 5000
\end{array}
$$

Units required for a target profit of $120000$:

$$
\text{Units} = \frac{400000 + 120000}{20} = 26000
$$

Profit at budgeted sales:

$$
\text{Profit} = (25000 \times 20) - 400000 = 100000
$$

The same profit follows from the margin of safety, since $5000 \times 20 = 100000$.

</Solution>
