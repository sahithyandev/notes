Management accounting is concerned with the provision and use of accounting information to managers within an organization, to facilitate their decision making and management control functions.

See [Introduction to Accounting](/s5/business-economics-and-financial-accounting/accounting/introduction) for how it compares to financial accounting.

## Activities

- Explaining production and non-production costs and how they are reported in the financial statements.
- Computing the cost of providing a service or manufacturing a product.
- Determining the behavior of costs and expenses as activity levels change.
- Analyzing cost-volume-profit relationships within a company.
- Assisting management in profit planning and budgeting.
- Providing a basis for controlling costs and expenses by comparing actual results with planned objectives and standard costs.
- Accumulating and presenting relevant data for management decision making.
