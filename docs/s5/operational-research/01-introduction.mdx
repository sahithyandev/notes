Operational research applies mathematical models and algorithms to decision making under constraints.

The module covers 4 main areas:

- Linear programming  
  Modelling and solving optimization problems with linear objectives and constraints.
- Game theory  
  Decision making between competing agents with conflicting objectives.
- Integer programming  
  Optimization problems where decision variables are restricted to integers.
- Network optimization  
  Optimization problems modelled as flows or paths on a graph.

## Hyperplane

Generalization of a line (from $\mathbb{R}^2$) to $\mathbb{R}^n$

$$
\{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : a^Tx = c\}
$$

Here $a \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$.

## Half-Space

A hyperplane $a^Tx = c$ divides $\mathbb{R}^n$ into two half-spaces, the sets of points on either side of it.

- Open half-space  
  $a^Tx < c$ or $a^Tx > c$. Excludes the boundary hyperplane.
- Closed half-space  
  $a^Tx \le c$ or $a^Tx \ge c$. Includes the boundary hyperplane.

Every linear constraint in $n$ decision variables is a closed half-space. The feasible region is the intersection of all constraint half-spaces.

In $\mathbb{R}^2$, a half-space is called a half-plane.
