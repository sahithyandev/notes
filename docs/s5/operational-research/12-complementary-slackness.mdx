Relates the optimal primal and dual solutions: at optimality, either a constraint is binding (0 slack/surplus) or its corresponding variable in the other problem is 0, never both non-zero.

$$
x_j \cdot (\text{dual constraint } j \text{ slack}) = 0 \quad \text{for every primal variable } x_j
$$

$$
y_i \cdot (\text{primal constraint } i \text{ slack}) = 0 \quad \text{for every dual variable } y_i
$$

Used to recover one problem's optimal solution directly from the other's, without re-solving.
