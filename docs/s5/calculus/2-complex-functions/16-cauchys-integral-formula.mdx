Let $f$ be analytic in a simply connected domain $D$, and let $C$ be a positively oriented simple closed contour lying in $D$. If $z_0$ lies inside $C$,

$$
f(z_0) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_C \frac{f(z)}{z - z_0}\,\text{d}z
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\int_C \frac{f(z)}{z - z_0}\,\text{d}z = 2\pi i f(z_0)
$$

## For Derivatives

Let $f$ be analytic in a simply connected domain $D$, and let $C$ be a positively oriented simple closed contour lying in $D$. If $z_0$ lies inside $C$, for $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$,

$$
f^{(n)}(z_0) = \frac{n!}{2\pi i}\int_C \frac{f(z)}{(z-z_0)^{n+1}}\,\text{d}z
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\int_C \frac{f(z)}{(z-z_0)^{n+1}}\,\text{d}z = \frac{2\pi i}{n!}f^{(n)}(z_0)
$$

<Note>

Analyticity of $f$ on $D$ forces $f$ to be infinitely differentiable there, since $f^{(n)}(z_0)$ exists for every $n$.

</Note>
