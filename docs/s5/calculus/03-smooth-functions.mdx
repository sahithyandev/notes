$\vec{r}(t)$ is smooth on an open interval $I$ **iff**:

- Differentiable
  $\vec{r}$ is differentiable on $I$.
- Continuous derivative
  $\vec{r}'$ is continuous on $I$.
- Non-vanishing derivative
  $\vec{r}'(t) \ne \vec{0}$ for all $t \in I$.

A curve traced by $\vec{r}(t)$ may fail to be smooth where $\vec{r}'$ is not continuous or where $\vec{r}'(t) = \vec{0}$.

Examples:

- $\vec{r}(t) = \langle 6\cos t - \cos 6t, 6\sin t - \sin 6t \rangle$, $0 \le t \le 2\pi$, is not smooth at the points between its petals.
