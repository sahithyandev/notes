For a particle moving along a curve $C$ traced by $\boldsymbol{r}(t)$, the derivative of $\boldsymbol{r}(t)$ carries a physical interpretation used throughout this module.

## Velocity

$$
\vec{v}(t) = \boldsymbol{r}'(t)
$$

$\vec{v}(t)$ is tangent to $C$ and points in the direction of motion.

## Speed

$$
v(t) = \lVert \vec{v}(t) \rVert = \lVert \boldsymbol{r}'(t) \rVert
$$

<Note>

Arc length is the accumulation of speed over time: $L = \int_a^b v(t) \, \text{d}t$.

</Note>

## Acceleration

$$
\vec{a}(t) = \vec{v}'(t) = \boldsymbol{r}''(t)
$$

$\vec{a}(t)$ measures how the velocity vector changes, both in magnitude (speed) and in direction.
