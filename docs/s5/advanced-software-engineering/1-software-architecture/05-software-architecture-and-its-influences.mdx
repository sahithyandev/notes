## What Is Software Architecture?

Software architecture encompasses the structure of large software systems.

It provides an **abstract view** of the system by:

- Eliminating implementation, algorithm, and data representation details.
- Focusing on the behaviour and interaction of **black-box elements**.

The architecture of a software system consists of its software elements, the externally visible properties of those elements, and the relationships among them.

## Architecture Business Cycle

The relationship between software architecture and its environment forms a cycle called the **Architecture Business Cycle (ABC)**.

- Software architecture is influenced by **technical, business, and social factors**.
- The resulting architecture also affects these environments.

## Where Do Architectures Come From?

Software architectures are the result of a set of **business and technical decisions**.

Requirements specify some, but not all, of the desired properties of the final system.

Failure to satisfy both documented and undocumented constraints can cause problems.

## Architectural Influences

### Stakeholders

- Different stakeholders have different concerns and goals.
- Some stakeholder goals may be contradictory.

### Development Organization

The organization influences architecture through:

- Immediate business goals
- Long-term business goals
- Staff skills
- Schedule
- Budget

### Architect's Background and Experience

Architects use their previous experience to:

- Repeat successful approaches.
- Avoid repeating past failures.

### Technical Environment

The technical environment influences architecture through:

- Standard industry practices
- Common software engineering techniques

## Managing Architectural Influences

Architects need to understand the **nature, source, and priority of project constraints** as early as possible.

Architects should:

- Identify and actively engage stakeholders.
- Understand stakeholder needs and expectations.
- Use architecture reviews.
- Use iterative prototyping.

## Architecture as a Feedback Cycle

Business goals, product requirements, architect experience, software architecture, and field systems form a **cycle with feedback loops**.

This cycle can help a business:

- Handle growth.
- Expand into new enterprise areas.
- Take advantage of previous investments in architecture and system development.
