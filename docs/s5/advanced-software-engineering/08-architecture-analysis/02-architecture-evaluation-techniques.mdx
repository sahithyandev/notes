## Presentations

A **presentation** is an informal explanation of the proposed architecture to stakeholders.

The presentation should be carefully structured so that the audience considers the implications of architectural decisions.

### Purpose of Presentations

- Communicate the proposed architecture to stakeholders.
- Help explain and promote the architecture.
- Encourage stakeholders to think about important architectural issues.

### Limitations of Presentations

Only a **shallow level of analysis** is possible through presentations.

Presentations are useful for communication, but they do not provide the same depth of analysis as more structured evaluation techniques.

## Formal Reviews and Structured Walkthroughs

A **formal review** involves gathering a group of people to examine a document page by page.

The purpose is to identify concerns, discuss them, and agree on any actions that need to be taken.

### Formal Review Process

A formal review generally involves:

1. Reviewing the document page by page.
2. Raising comments and concerns.
3. Discussing the concerns as a group.
4. Agreeing on the actions that need to be taken, if any.

### Participant Roles

Each attendee is assigned a role before the review meeting.

Examples include:

- **Moderator** Organizes and manages the review.
- **Presenter** Presents the material being reviewed.
- **Reviewers** Examine the material and raise concerns.

### Preparation for a Review

The materials to be reviewed are typically sent to reviewers a few days before the meeting.

These materials may include:

- Specifications
- Design documents
- Code

Providing the materials in advance allows participants to prepare for the review.

## Comparing the Two Techniques

| Technique         | Main Purpose                                               | Key Characteristic                                |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Presentation**  | Communicate the proposed architecture to stakeholders.     | Informal; supports only shallow analysis.         |
| **Formal Review** | Examine documents, discuss concerns, and agree on actions. | Structured group examination with assigned roles. |
