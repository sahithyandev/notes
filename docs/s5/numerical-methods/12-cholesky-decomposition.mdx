$A$ is [positive definite](/s5/numerical-methods/positive-definite-matrices) **iff** $A$ factors as $A = LL^T$, where $L$ is lower triangular with non-zero diagonal entries. This factorization is the Cholesky decomposition.

The triangular factors are transposes of each other.

For row $k$:

$$
l_{ki} = \frac{a_{ki} - \sum\limits_{j=1}^{i-1} l_{ij}l_{kj}}{l_{ii}}, \quad \text{for } i = 1, 2, \ldots, k-1
$$

$$
l_{kk} = \sqrt{a_{kk} - \sum_{j=1}^{k-1} l_{kj}^2}
$$

Examples:

Apply Cholesky decomposition to

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 15 & 55 \\ 15 & 55 & 225 \\ 55 & 225 & 979 \end{pmatrix}
$$
