## Materials

Materials can be divided into 4 sub-classifications.

- Metals
- Ceramics
- Polymers
- Composites

Materials can also be categorized based on their usage or their
functionalities.

<Note>

Previously wood was considered as a separate classification, along with the
above-mentioned ones.

</Note>

## Property

The response given by a material to a specific stimulus.

- [Mechanical properties](/s1/properties-of-materials/mechanical-properties/introduction)  
  Response to stress.
- [Electrical properties](/s1/properties-of-materials/mechanical-properties/introduction)  
  Response to an electric field.
- Magnetic properties  
  Response to a magnetic field.
- Optical properties  
  Response to electromagnetic or light radiation.

## Definitions

### Anisotropic

Different properties in different directions.

### Isotropic

Same properties in all different directions.
