Suppose $w=f(z)$ where $z,w \in \mathbb{C}$. Input and output points are marked
in 2 separate complex planes.

![Complex function mapping](./images/complex-function.jpg)

Here:

- $D$ - domain of $f$
- $D'$ - codomain of $f$

### Image

Image of $f$ is the set:

$$
\big\{
f(z)\;|\;z\in D
\big\}
$$

## Cartesian Form

$$
f(z) = u(x,y) + iv(x,y)
$$

Here $u, v$ are real functions.
