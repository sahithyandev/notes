## Distance Between 2 Skew Lines

$$
\text{distance}=\lvert{\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\underline{n}}\rvert
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="\underline{n}">the unit normal to both $l_1,l_2$</Sym>
  <Sym s="A, B">points lying on each line</Sym>
</Symbols>
