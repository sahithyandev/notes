Resistance, in terms of physical dimensions:

<Formula>

$$
R = \frac{\rho l}{A}
$$

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="l">length</Sym>
  <Sym s="A">cross-sectional area</Sym>
  <Sym s="\rho">
    [resistivity](/s1/properties-of-materials/electrical-properties/introduction/#resistivity)
  </Sym>
</Symbols>

</Formula>

If a voltage $ V $ is applied across a conductor, then a given current $ I $
will flow through the conductor $ V \propto I $. The proportionality constant is
called resistance $ R $.

$$
R = \frac{V}{I}
$$
