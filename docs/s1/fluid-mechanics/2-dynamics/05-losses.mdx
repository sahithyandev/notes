Energy loss in fluid motion is directly proportional to $v^2$ where $v$ is the
velocity.

## Types

### Local Loss

Occurs when there is a sudden change in flow.

<Formula>

$$
K_L
\frac{v^2}{2g}
$$

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="K_L">local loss coefficient</Sym>
  <Sym s="v">velocity</Sym>
  <Sym s="g">gravitational acceleration</Sym>
</Symbols>

</Formula>

### Friction Loss

Occurs because of

- viscosity
- friction between fluid and pipe wall

When the fluid is under turbulent flow:

<Formula>

$$
\lambda
\frac{L}{D}
\frac{v^2}{2g}
$$

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="\lambda">friction factor</Sym>
  <Sym s="L">length of the pipe</Sym>
  <Sym s="D">diameter of the pipe</Sym>
  <Sym s="v">velocity</Sym>
  <Sym s="g">gravitational acceleration</Sym>
</Symbols>

</Formula>
