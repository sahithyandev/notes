## Continuity Equation

From the conservation of mass law, the below equation can be derived for an
incompressible fluid:

$$
Q= Av
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="Q">Flow rate</Sym>
  <Sym s="A">Cross-sectional area</Sym>
  <Sym s="v">velocity</Sym>
</Symbols>

## Bernoulli's Equation

For an incompressible fluid in steady flow, total head on a point is constant
throughout a [streamline](/s1/fluid-mechanics/dynamics/introduction/#streamline).
Can be derived from the conservation of energy law.

$$
\text{Total head }H= z + \frac{P}{\rho g} + \frac{v^2}{2g}
$$

Here:

- $z$ - Datum head. Height to the point from a reference level.
- $\frac{P}{\rho g}$ -
  [Pressure head](/s1/fluid-mechanics/statics/hydrostatic-pressure/#pressure-head).
- $\frac{v^2}{2g}$ - Velocity head. Kinetic energy per unit weight.

<Note>

- Head is the energy per unit weight.
- Piezometric head is equal to $\frac{P^*}{\rho g}$ where $P^*$ is the
  piezometric pressure.

</Note>

## Steady Flow Momentum Equation

For an incompressible fluid under steady flow:

$$
F_S = \dot{M_o}-\dot{M_i}
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="F_S">Force exerted on the fluid within the control volume</Sym>
  <Sym s="\dot{M_o}">Rate of change of momentum of the inflow fluid</Sym>
  <Sym s="\dot{M_i}">Rate of change of momentum of the outflow fluid</Sym>
</Symbols>

$$
\dot{M} = \rho Q v
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="\dot{M}">rate of change of momentum</Sym>
  <Sym s="\rho">density</Sym>
  <Sym s="Q">flow rate</Sym>
  <Sym s="v">velocity</Sym>
</Symbols>

## Power

$$
P=\gamma Q H
$$

Here:

<Symbols>
  <Sym s="\gamma">specific weight of the fluid</Sym>
  <Sym s="Q">flow rate</Sym>
  <Sym s="H">total head</Sym>
</Symbols>
