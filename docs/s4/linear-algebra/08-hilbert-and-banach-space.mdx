Cauchy sequences can be defined on metric spaces.

### Complete

A set is said to be complete **iff** all [Cauchy sequences](/s1/mathematics/real-analysis/cauchy-sequence) approach a limit in the set.

## Hilbert Space

A complete inner product space.

## Banach Space

A complete normed space.
