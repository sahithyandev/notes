## Security

Arise due to the large number of connected devices and distributed architectures.

### Key Issues

- Device access protection
- Physical security of devices
- Lack of visibility and control
- Malware attacks
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks

## Privacy

As IoT devices collect and transmit personal data, privacy concerns arise regarding how this data is handled and protected.

### Issues

- Unauthorized access to devices
- Data exposure
- Personal information leakage
- Privacy violations

## Technological Challenges

IoT technologies face challenges due to evolving standards and infrastructure dependencies.

### Issues

- Rapid technological changes
- Dependency on power, networks, and cloud services
- Infrastructure limitations
- Relatively immature ecosystem
