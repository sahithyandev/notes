Probability that an adverse circumstance will occur.

3 categories:

- Project risks — affect schedule or resources.
- Product risks — affect quality or performance.
- Business risks — affect the developing or procuring organisation.

## Risk Management

4 sequential and circular stages.

1. **Risk identification** → produces list of potential risks.
2. **Risk analysis** → produces prioritized risk list.
3. **Risk planning** → produces avoidance and contingency plans.
4. **Risk monitoring** → produces risk assessment; feeds back to identification.

### Risk Identification

Identifying a list of risks. Identification sources could be:

- team activity
- individual manager experience

A checklist of risk categories helps enumeration:

- Technology risks
- People risks
- Organizational risks
- Requirements risks
- Estimation risks

### Risk Analysis

Assessing probability and seriousness of each identified risk. Usually 3 or 4 or 5 levels of probability scale and consequence scale are used.

### Risk Planning

Developing strategies for each prioritized risks.

Strategies can be split into 3 types:

- Avoidance strategies  
  Reduce probability the risk arises.
- Minimization strategies  
  Reduce impact if the risk occurs.
- Contingency plans  
  Predefined responses if the risk materializes.

### Risk Monitoring

Regular assessment of each risk throughout the project. Assess whether the probability has changed (increased or decreased), whether consequences have changed.
