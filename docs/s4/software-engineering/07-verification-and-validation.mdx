## Verification

The process of checking whether the software conforms to the specification.

Both static and dynamic verifications are done in the V&V process.

## Validation

The process of checking whether the software meets user expectations.

## V&V Confidence

Aim of V&V is to establish confidence that the system is _fit for purpose_.

Depends on:

- Software purpose  
  The level of confidence depends on how critical the software is to an organisation.
- User expectations  
  Users may have low expectations of certain kinds of software.
- Marketing environment  
  Getting a product to market early may be more important than finding defects in the program.
