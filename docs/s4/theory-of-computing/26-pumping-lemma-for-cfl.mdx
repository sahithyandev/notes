Let $L$ be a CFL. Then $\exists n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\forall u \in L$ satisfying $|u| \ge n$, there exist strings $v, w, x, y, z$ satisfying:

- $u = vwxyz$
- $|wy| > 0$
- $|wxy| \le n$
- $\forall m \ge 0$, $vw^mxy^mz \in L$

## Using the Lemma to Prove Non-CFL

To show $L$ is not a CFL, assume $L$ is a CFL and derive a contradiction.

Standard proof structure:

1. Assume $L$ is a CFL with pumping length $n$.
2. Choose a string $u \in L$ with $|u| \ge n$.
3. Consider all possible decompositions $u = vwxyz$ satisfying $|wy| > 0$ and $|wxy| \le n$.
4. Show that for every such decomposition, some $vw^mxy^mz \notin L$ for some $m \ge 0$.
5. Contradiction. $L$ is not a CFL.

## Example

Prove $L = \{a^n b^n c^n \mid n \ge 1\}$ is not a CFL.

Assume $L$ is a CFL with pumping length $p$. Choose $u = a^p b^p c^p$, so $|u| = 3p \ge p$.

Any decomposition $u = vwxyz$ with $|wxy| \le p$ means $wxy$ spans at most 2 of the 3 symbol types (cannot reach from the $a$-block to the $c$-block in $p$ characters).

Case 1: $wxy$ lies entirely in $a^p b^p$. Pumping increases the count of $a$s or $b$s (or both) but not $c$s. The pumped string has unequal counts.

Case 2: $wxy$ lies entirely in $b^p c^p$. Pumping increases $b$s or $c$s but not $a$s. Unequal counts.

In all cases, $vw^2xy^2z \notin L$. Contradiction. $L$ is not a CFL.

## Ogden’s Lemma

A generalization of the pumping lemma for CFL. Allows marking “distinguished” positions in $u$. Guarantees $w$ or $y$ contains at least 1 distinguished position.

Useful when the standard lemma is insufficient to force a contradiction. Every CFL satisfies Ogden’s lemma; it strictly subsumes the pumping lemma.
