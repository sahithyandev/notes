Any real-world computation can be translated into an equivalent Turing machine computation. A TM is a general model of computation.

Formerly known as Church's thesis. First formulated by Alonzo Church. Treated as a conjecture.

Accepted because:

- No counterexample proposed in 90+ years.
- All other theoretical computation models proven equivalent to TMs.
- Enhanced TMs provide no additional computing power.
- TM operations capture all steps essential to human computation.
