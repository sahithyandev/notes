Aka. CSG. An unrestricted grammar where every production $\alpha \to \beta$ satisfies $|\beta| \geq |\alpha|$.

Equivalent form: $\alpha A \beta \to \alpha X \beta$ where:

- $X \neq \Lambda$
- $A \in V$
- $\alpha, \beta, X \in (V \cup \Sigma)^*$

## Context-Sensitive Language

Aka. CSL. Languages generated by a CSG.

A language is context-sensitive **iff** it can be generated by a grammar in which every production has the form:

$$
αAβ → αXβ
$$

where $α, β$ and $X$ are strings of non-terminals and/or terminals, $X ≠Λ$ and $A$ is a non-terminal.
