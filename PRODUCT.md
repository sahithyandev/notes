# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students and independent learners use the site to study structured technical notes. They need to find a topic quickly, build understanding in sequence, and revisit material without visual friction.

## Product Purpose

Notes is a static academic reference organised by semester and module. It turns concise Markdown source material into accessible, navigable study pages with equations, worked examples, diagrams, and downloadable supporting material.

## Brand Personality

Precise, scholarly, and calm. The site should feel like a carefully maintained personal knowledge base, not a generic documentation portal or a course-selling platform.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like chrome, marketing-page theatrics, gamified learning patterns, and dense textbook scans. Avoid visual decoration that competes with mathematical notation or the note hierarchy.

## Design Principles

- Put comprehension before ornament.
- Make the structure of an argument immediately scannable.
- Use diagrams and examples to reveal relationships that prose alone hides.
- Preserve a stable reading environment across long technical notes.
- Keep source authoring simple while allowing rich rendered explanations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard-access expectations. Preserve semantic headings, tables, captions, and text alternatives. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate a distinction.
